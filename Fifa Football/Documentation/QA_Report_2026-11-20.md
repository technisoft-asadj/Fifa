# Football Simulator v0.2.0 — QA report (2026-11-20)

**Device:** Infinix X6881, Android 14, arm64, 1080x2436, 7.8 GB RAM. **Build:** `FifaFootball.apk` (0.2.0), installed over adb.
**How it was tested:** driven over adb (taps and swipes), screen video and screenshots, logcat, plus a read of the gameplay scripts.
**Confidence tags:** `[DEVICE]` seen on the phone, `[CODE]` found in the scripts, `[CODE+DEVICE]` both, `[CHECK]` suspected, needs hands-on confirmation.

**Limits of this pass**
- adb cannot hold two fingers at once, so stick + button at the same time was not tested. Please check that by hand.
- Someone rebuilt and reinstalled the APK on this phone at 01:17 while I was testing. That ended one session and explains a profile that looked reset (Player / Level 1 / 0 coins) afterwards. Neither is counted as a game bug.
- A full 3-minute match was played to full time. The first half-time and full-time screens were seen; Online host/join with a second device, Cup/League/Season flows and Training were not played through.

## Summary
- **Stability:** no crashes, no Unity exceptions in logcat, steady 59-60 fps with 22 players, about 450 MB memory, about 44°C. Background/resume and the Android Back key work.
- **Main problems:** touch aiming has no assist, tap-shots are weak, players stack on top of each other, the camera shows empty void and tilts oddly, several HUD and menu overlaps, and the match flow has dead ends (no way back to the menu from full time or pause; Restart/Rematch takes about 12 s through a flash of the main menu with placeholder data).

## A. Gameplay bugs and realism problems

| ID | Sev | Tag | Where | Problem | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | High | CODE | Touch input | `TouchControls.AimWorld` is always zero, so a shot or pass goes where the player is running or facing. There is no aim toward goal and no pass target selection. Passing to a team-mate needs you to face him. | Auto-aim shots at the goal (with some error), auto-target passes to the best team-mate inside a cone around the stick direction. |
| G2 | High | CODE | Shoot | A quick tap releases with `max(0.25, charge)` = 25% of max shot speed. Phone players tap, so most shots are weak. There is no visible power bar for the 0.8 s charge. | Show a charge ring on the Shoot button, raise the minimum tap power, or make a tap a normal shot. |
| G3 | Med | CODE | Pass | Fixed pass power of 55% regardless of distance. Short and long passes feel identical, and long passes fall short. | Scale power with distance to the target (the AI already does this). |
| G4 | Med | CODE | Pass/Shoot | The ball must be within 1.1 m for a strike. A press just outside that is silently consumed: no sound, no animation, no feedback. | Buffer the press for about 0.2 s, or show a miss animation. |
| G5 | Med | CODE+DEVICE | Players | Two team-mates often stand on exactly the same spot (seen as pairs on both wings, for both teams, for the whole match on the radar). Formation z positions are scaled by 1.6 and then clamped, so several slots collapse onto the same line. | Add a separation force between team-mates and use unclamped, distinct slot positions. |
| G6 | Med | CODE | Dribble | `KeepBallClose` steers the ball toward a point ahead of the runner whenever he last touched it. The ball feels glued. It cannot be knocked off by running into it, only by a successful tackle. | Weaken the assist, and let sharp turns and sprint speed lose the ball. |
| G7 | Med | CODE | Tackle | Win chance is 0.55 + strength/400 whatever the angle or whether the ball is covered. Failed tackles that touch a player are a foul (85% from behind), so spam-tapping Tackle gives away many free kicks. A 0.9 s cooldown applies. | Make the chance depend on angle and ball distance, and show a clear cooldown on the button. |
| G8 | Med | CODE | Player switching | Auto-switch only happens when a team-mate is more than 6 m closer to the ball. The Switch button chooses the player closest to the ball, not the one in the direction you are pushing. While defending, the controlled player is often the wrong one. | Switch toward the ball side and the nearest defender in the stick direction. |
| G9 | Med | CODE | Match clock | The match clock keeps running during the 3 s goal pause and restart holds, so celebrations eat playing time. The "3 min" match shows a 0-90 clock with no label. | Pause the clock during goal pauses and label it as match time. |
| G10 | Low | CODE | Goalkeepers | A human can never control a keeper (`ClosestOutfielderToBall`), but the Tackle button turns into a dive/Clear for keeper contexts. | G11 | Low | DEVICE | AI | After 18 game minutes of standing still the Away side scored, so the AI does attack. ## D. Things that work well
- Online screen shows the Wi-Fi address and validates an empty Join.
- Controls screen options (stick type, hand, size, opacity, auto sprint, Move buttons).
- Context-sensitive buttons (Shoot/Pass vs Clear/Tackle) switch correctly.
- Goal, GOAL banner, score and full-time reward screen work.
- Performance and stability.

## E. Suggested fix order
1. G1 and G2 (touch aim assist, tap-shot power and charge indicator).
2. U4, U5, U6 (menu/quit/restart flow).
3. G5 (player stacking) and C1/C2 (stadium void and camera tilt).
4. U1–U3 (overlay and label overlaps), G11 (check possession stat).
5. Everything else.
